Privacy Policy

One Thing – One Task. One Block of Time.

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains how One Thing handles information when you use our Android application.

1. Information Collection

One Thing does not require account registration and does not collect or transmit personal information to external servers.

2. Local Data Storage

Information such as tasks, focus sessions, productivity records and related app preferences is stored locally on your device.

One Thing does not provide cloud storage or online synchronization.

3. Data Sharing

One Thing does not sell, rent or share users' personal information with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

One Thing does not use advertising networks, analytics services or third-party tracking services.

5. Data Security

Your application data is stored locally on your device. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device.

Android system-level backup settings may affect whether application data is included in device backups.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete locally stored application data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling One Thing. System-level backups may retain copies according to your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

One Thing does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or privacy practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
